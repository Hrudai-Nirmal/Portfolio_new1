# Realistic foliage assets

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool for this portfolio. Transparent PNG masters are retained here; optimized alpha WebP copies are served from public/foliage. No stock artwork was downloaded. These are raster assets, not SVGs. Each edge is an independent DOM layer for clockwise movement.

## Vine generation prompt

Create a production-ready isolated photorealistic botanical cutout asset with a genuinely transparent background. Single long gently curving horizontal tropical forest vine, growing from a thick woody stem on the RIGHT toward a finely tapered tendril at the LEFT. Wide landscape composition. Dense lush overlapping emerald and dark blue-green foliage all along its length, about 60 varied natural leaves: small ivy, elongated waxy leaves, delicate fern fronds, a few tiny muted violet flowers. Mostly leaves hang DOWNWARD from the horizontal stem. Varied imperfect leaf shapes and sizes, photographic fine veins, subtle specular highlights, realistic translucent edges, deep layered shadows, soft cool natural woodland light. Sophisticated real botanical photography, absolutely not cartoon, not vector, not plastic or 3D. Entire isolated vine fits within the canvas with transparent margins, no scenery, no ground, no black rectangle, no text. This will be rotated and composed along the four edges of a dark website. Dense irregular silhouette with side shoots, but long horizontal aspect ratio approximately 3:1. Save the finished asset as a local file.

## Fern generation prompt

Production botanical asset for a dark immersive forest website: one isolated lush cluster of overlapping real tropical fern fronds and a few large lanceolate rainforest leaves, with a handful of tiny muted violet wildflowers. Photorealistic botanical photography, extremely detailed leaf veins, natural imperfections, soft cool woodland light and deep emerald blue-green colors. Asymmetric fan grows from lower left corner outward toward upper right. Dense base, airy irregular delicate frond tips, transparent background with real alpha. Entire plant fits canvas with small transparent margins. No planter, no soil, no scenery, no text, no border, no cartoon or illustrated treatment. Square composition. The asset will sit in a corner of the viewport with the foliage pointing inward.

## Export

- Vine master: 2167 × 726 RGBA PNG.
- Fern master: 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNG.
- Sharp WebP quality 86, preserving alpha and source dimensions.
- Rotation, lighting, positioning, and mobile cropping are CSS properties. The source images are not flattened into a full-scene background.
